New Wage Code Compliance

Formulated at exactly 50% of CTC for Basic Salary. This satisfies statutory definitions of "wages" which mandate that allowances and other components must not exceed 50% of the total compensation structure.

Section 80CCD(2) Tax Rules

Under the New Tax Regime, employer's contribution to National Pension System (NPS) is deductible up to 14% of Basic Salary. Option 3 utilizes this rule to convert high-tax special allowances into tax-exempt retirement capital.

Section 87A Rebate & Marginal Relief

Income tax is completely zero if net taxable income is under ₹12 Lakhs. For income slightly above ₹12 Lakhs, Marginal Relief ensures that the tax payable does not exceed the income earned over ₹12 Lakhs.
